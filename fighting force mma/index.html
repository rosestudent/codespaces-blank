About Fighting Force MMA

Fighting Force MMA is a locally owned martial arts and fitness club based in Lenasia, Johannesburg.

The gym was established to provide a safe, welcoming and professional environment where people of all ages can learn self-defence, improve their fitness and build confidence.

Fighting Force MMA offers training in Mixed Martial Arts (MMA), boxing, kickboxing and other combat sports.

The gym welcomes beginners as well as experienced athletes and aims to develop discipline, respect and perseverance through structured training.

Our Vision

To become the leading martial arts and fitness academy in Lenasia by inspiring people of all ages to live healthier, more confident and disciplined lives through quality training.

Our Mission

  • Provide high-quality martial arts and fitness training.
  • Promote discipline, respect, confidence and perseverance.
  • Create a safe, welcoming and supportive environment.
  • Help members achieve their fitness and self-defence goals.
  • Make martial arts accessible and affordable.
